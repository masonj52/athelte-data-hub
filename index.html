      How the numbers work

      Deck
      Tempo

      Your readiness score from 0 to 100, shown as 90 plus half the score. A score of 60 reads 120. The colour runs red below 40, yellow in the 50s, green from 60 to 70, blue from 80 to 90 and white above 90.

      Readiness score

      Three readings are measured against your own 60 day baseline and blended: HRV 45 percent, resting heart rate 30 percent with higher counted as worse, and last night’s sleep against a 7.5 hour need 25 percent. The effort line uses the sheet’s zones: 70 or more, 50 to 69, under 50.

      Up next

      Whichever of Push, Pull or Legs you tagged longest ago, once it has had two full days off. On a Legs day a run is added when the weekly distance goal is behind. Under a score of 50 the lift moves to tomorrow and an easy run or mobility takes its place. It only knows the lifts you tag on the Workouts tab.

      Run goal

      The suggestion is last week’s distance plus about 10 percent, never less than what you have already run this week. With no run last week it falls back to your four week average. The plus and minus buttons override it until you choose the suggestion again.

      Vitals verdict

      Each vital’s latest reading is compared with your own previous 30 days. A reading counts as off when it sits more than one standard deviation on the worse side: lower HRV, sleep or blood oxygen, higher resting heart rate, breathing rate or skin temperature.

      History

      The days in range are split into the higher and lower half for the chosen metric. The list shows which of sleep length, bedtime, deep sleep and the previous day’s training differ most between the two halves. It shows what moves together, not what causes what.

      HRV

      One value per night: the variation between heartbeats while you sleep. Reading the trend against a personal baseline follows Plews and colleagues, 2013. The measure is defined in the 1996 Task Force standards.

      Calories

      Burned is everything for the day, resting included, as Google Health totals it. Active is the part above resting. Workout calories are the active burn inside a recorded workout. Walks under 20 minutes are left out of the workout list.
